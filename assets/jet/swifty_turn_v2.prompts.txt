BLUE GENERATION
Use case: identity-preserve. Production sprite atlas for smooth game animation. Reference image 1 is the canonical Swifty character and sporty jet ski design; reference image 2 supplies matching low-angle directions. Create ONE transparent-background landscape sprite sheet, 1536x1024 or larger, EXACTLY SIX equal columns and FOUR equal rows, TWENTY-FOUR complete sprites total. Every cell is the SAME BLUE jet ski with the SAME Swifty riding it. Not four color variants: ONLY BLUE in all 24 cells. Lock the original white fluffy duck, feather tuft, expressive blue eyes, orange beak and webbed feet, yellow hoodie, blue shorts, brown diagonal satchel; hands stay gripping the same handlebars, seated naturally. Lock the chunky rounded BLUE jet ski with dark navy underside, white swoosh band and yellow lightning decal. Preserve the polished original illustrated game style, not a primitive model. Camera always 15 degrees above water, NEVER overhead. This is ONE smooth clockwise full turn in small FIFTEEN DEGREE increments; every neighboring cell must have a subtly different yaw, including in-between poses, not repeated cardinal directions. Row-major angles: row 1 = 0,15,30,45,60,75; row 2 = 90,105,120,135,150,165; row 3 = 180,195,210,225,240,255; row 4 = 270,285,300,315,330,345. At 0 degrees boat bow and smiling duck face point straight toward viewer. At 90 degrees bow points screen-right, right-facing SIDE PROFILE. At 180 degrees bow points straight away and duck's BACK is visible. At 270 degrees bow points screen-left, left-facing SIDE PROFILE. Keep rotation physically coherent, heading changes to boat AND duck together. Same lighting, duck size, jet ski size, camera elevation, framing and waterline in all 24 cells. The torso, hands, face and silhouette must not change scale between frames. Put a small attached turquoise splash at each waterline, no large wakes. Strict equal grid; every sprite centered in its own square cell, same baseline at 88% cell height, with at least 7% fully transparent padding on all four edges of EACH cell. No sprites cross cell boundaries. No text, angle numbers, grid lines or checkerboard. TRUE alpha transparency. Preserve original design identity exactly.
